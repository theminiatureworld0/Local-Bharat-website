# Local Bharat — Discover Local. Grow Local.

> **Local Business Discovery, Growth & Commerce Platform**
> Complete Business Model, Product Requirements & Execution Blueprint (Version 3.0)
> Targeting Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities in India (Jaipur, Pune, Nashik, and regional growth hubs).

---

## 🚀 Five Business Pillars

1. **Local Discovery Marketplace** — Nearby shops, local service providers, professionals, manufacturers, and artisans in your city and locality.
2. **Local Business Growth Centre** — Digital storefronts, enquiry inboxes, structured quotation builders, customer follow-up tracking, and marketing tools.
3. **Custom Requirements & Quotation Marketplace** — Submit a requirement (e.g. house compound wall, 500 custom cartons, solar rooftop, handcrafted pooja mandir) and compare 3–4 itemised local quotes.
4. **Product & Service Commerce** — Direct local product ordering, doorstep technician visits, and transparent fulfilment.
5. **B2B Business Network** — Wholesalers, raw material suppliers, distributors, and bulk institutional procurement.

## 📱 Platform Interfaces

1. **Public Website & Discovery** — Fast, SEO-optimized public portal for customer acquisition, categories, listings, and custom requirement postings.
2. **Customer Account (`/account`)** — Dedicated dashboard for order tracking, doorstep appointments, quotation reviews, saved vendors, and store onboarding.
3. **Vendor Store Onboarding** — Free store registration, catalogue management, and direct customer lead generation.
4. **Quotation Engine (`/projects`)** — Real local project case studies and itemised scope matching.
